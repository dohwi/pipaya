SUIT
Copyright (c) 2022, SUNN (http://sun.fo/suit), with Reserved Font Name SUIT.
Source: https://github.com/sun-typeface/SUIT/blob/main/fonts/variable/ttf/SUIT-Variable.ttf
suit-variable.woff2 is a WOFF2-compressed copy of the complete variable TTF.
Compression preserves the original glyphs, names, copyright, license metadata,
weight axis (100–900), and modification timestamp. No subsetting or redesign.
It includes all 11,172 precomposed Hangul syllables, so user-entered Korean
and future examples do not depend on the site's content-specific font subset.

Fonts are served from /fonts/; no runtime requests to font CDNs are required.
